MEASURE → ISOLATE → VERIFY → REPAIR → PROVE

Start with evidence. Separate hydraulic, electrical, mechanical and control-system causes before changing parts or parameters.

PUMPS // HYDRAULICS

  • Duty point, curve & BEP
  • TDH / static / friction
  • NPSHa vs NPSHr
  • Cavitation & suction conditions
  • Seals, sleeves, bearings & alignment
  • Vertical turbine & centrifugal systems

VFD // CONTROLS

  • Input/output voltage & current
  • Hz, RPM & motor data
  • Pressure transducer scaling
  • PID hunting & response
  • Accel / decel / min speed
  • Drive vs motor vs hydraulic fault

CENTRAL CONTROL

  • Learned vs measured flow
  • Hydraulic tree / capacity
  • Flowmeter scaling
  • Pressure-flow correlation
  • Decoder / two-wire diagnostics
  • Sequence & runtime evidence
