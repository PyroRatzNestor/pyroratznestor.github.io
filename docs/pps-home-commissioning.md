# PPS Home commissioning record

The dashboard's **Checklists** tab opens the PPS Home commissioning companion.
It is a paraphrased, local record based on owner-supplied screenshots of
**PPS QF-16.5 Start-Up and Commissioning Form, Rev B, 01/22/25, pages 1-4**.
The official form is the controlling document; this portal is neither a PPS
form replica nor a technical review or work procedure.

The record includes the form header, three operator-training attendee slots,
completion sign-off fields, separate PPS/PAS control-panel and PPS pump-skid
applicability choices, pump counts, the pre-power and power-on Done/N/A rows,
eight pump/motor settings slots, the voltage/pressure table, the three photo
groups, and commissioning notes. A `*` marker is retained where the source
marks work duplicated in another section. Choosing No for the panel skips its
checklist; choosing No for the skid skips its checklist but leaves the
Pump/Motor and Settings Table available.

The companion autosaves on this browser and device. Use its JSON export/import
for backup or transfer; this is separate from the Notes `.TXT` backup. The
record can contain personal and site information, so protect exported files.
Photo rows track completion and an optional filename/reference only; images are
not uploaded. Typed signature entries are not electronic signatures. Complete
formal acceptance on the official form.

The form contains electrical, pressure, pump-operation and adjustment checks.
This companion only records status and supplied values. Follow the current PPS
form, exact equipment manuals, employer/site procedures and qualified-person
requirements. In particular, the supplied form assigns phase-reversal
correction to the contracted electrician. Do not enter PPS/PAS activation or
lockout codes in the portal.
