Source policy & limitations

The dashboard labels source editions, pages, assumptions and unresolved conflicts where available. A filename or upload year is not proof of a document's edition. Verify the current manufacturer's documentation before relying on a limit.

  • Model and configuration matter. Check the full model, nozzle, voltage, pressure rating, firmware and active control path. Do not copy limits or PID values across families.
  • Unknown stays unknown. Pending and provisional references are labeled. Conflicting nominal values do not become an invented tolerance range.
  • Calculators are estimates or bounded lookups. Read each tool's units, inputs and limitations. A voltage-drop screen is not electrical-code compliance or decoder-path approval.
  • Catalog indexes are not service manuals. Model-family coverage does not reproduce every performance-table cell or identify every spare part. Original catalog PDFs are not redistributed here.
  • Access is not guaranteed. Manufacturer links can move or require login. A prior check date does not guarantee current availability.

Golf and landscape precipitation remain distinct. Shared hydraulic tools apply across systems; catalog placement identifies a source, not a usage restriction.

Before a change: obtain the applicable service procedure, preserve the commissioned baseline and parameter backup, and follow site authorization and safety requirements. Do not bypass protections or substitute factory defaults for the commissioned setup.

Use evidence and the installed equipment's documentation together.
